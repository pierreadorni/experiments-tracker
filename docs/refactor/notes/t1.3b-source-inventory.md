# T1.3b source inventory and card candidates

Status: read-only investigation; **not design approval**. Inspected source at
`18e37fd9ee8459baa07bc1c090173e790fe318ca` (2026-11-14). Estimates below are
approximate **SOURCE ONLY** changed lines. Remeasure each card with tests before
delegation; keep source plus tests below 500 lines and split if needed. No source
or test files were changed for this inventory.

T1.3a adds `core.obligations.open/satisfy/abandon` with caller-owned transaction
semantics. Its immutable identity includes `owner`; a terminal row cannot reopen,
and changing owner on an open ID is rejected. Every integration must therefore
settle the old obligation and use a new stable ID when responsibility changes.
The coordinator must decide whether/how a source can hand off owners, especially
uncertain agent actions, before implementation. Do not loosen the API implicitly.

## Writer and transaction inventory

`agent_actions` has inserts in `workflow.py` at 741 and 2157, `controller.py` at
651, `conversation.py` at 417, and `store.py` at 468 and 1373. State-changing SQL
is broader than `Workflow._action_state`: workflow at 798, 809, 3625, 3759, 3970,
4020 and 4112; store at 1250; controller at 552; conversation at 468, 525 and
548; cancellation and legacy archival helpers also settle actions. Input, thread,
turn, interrupt, and control-revision updates are identity-preserving metadata,
not terminal transitions, but creation and every state write must be checked.

Job state writers include workflow submit/reconcile/complete/failure paths;
iteration's `agent` job insert and completion; store retry failure; cancellation's
submitted/cancelled paths; steering's scheduler observation; and
`legacy_archive.py` exact-identity settlement. Poll timestamps and scheduler
observations alone are not terminal outcomes. Transfer state writers are limited
to `remote.py` pending/complete and store cancellation. `report_versions` writers
are store insert/state, artifact registration (`publishing`), final workflow
completion/failure, and cancellation. The source transition, obligation update,
and existing `Store.emit`/outbox write must share the caller's transaction.

Pending request creation/update often goes through `Store.update`, but expiration,
answer, reconciliation and direct `pending_request=NULL` paths also need review.
Plan review is JSON on `experiments`, not a dedicated row; every replacement,
approval, rejection, cancellation, and autopilot review write must be traced to the
same saved generation. Keep current `hold_reason` writes/projections unchanged in
T1.3b; derive them only in T1.3c.

## Coordinator decisions before implementation

1. **Owner handoff:** `owner` is part of immutable obligation identity. Decide how
   action uncertainty or other responsibility changes are represented: abandon
   the prior source obligation and open a distinct reconcile ID, retain one owner
   for the entire source lifecycle, or another explicit rule. Stable IDs must not
   be rebound to a new owner.
2. **Timer scope:** `agent_wakeups` are persisted timer-like work (`controller.py`
   creates, supersedes and fails them; `workflow.py` consumes wakeups). Decide
   whether they receive `timer` obligations keyed by `agent_wakeups.id` in T1.3b
   or a separate card. A timer should carry its saved `due_at`; firing/settlement
   must not dispatch a second action identity.
3. **Project scope:** `project_turns` and `autopilot_actions` have independent
   request/state paths in `knowledge.py`, `autopilot.py`, and
   `autopilot_controller.py`. Decide whether C2 T1.3b includes these project-level
   owner/agent waits or whether they need separate follow-up cards. Do not silently
   treat them as `agent_actions`.
4. **Publication boundary:** decide whether report readiness alone is the
   `publication` obligation, or whether remote artifact delivery (`artifacts.remote_published`)
   also needs a separately identified obligation. Existing outbox delivery remains
   its own saved identity and must not be conflated with report-version identity.
5. **Backfill/archival:** decide the bounded rollout mechanism for already-saved
   nonterminal records. Safe backfill can derive an open obligation from a saved
   action/job/transfer/request/review/report identity without changing source
   state or asserting external success. It must not redispatch or resolve an
   uncertain record, and must not resurrect terminal `legacy_contract_archived`
   execution. No guessed historical state should be backfilled.

Read-only schema compatibility and derived `hold_reason` handling belong to
T1.3c; watchdog liveness replacement belongs to T1.3d.
