## Final policy proposed after review

- Conversation > experiment > research > supervisor > title. Monitor shares
  experiment priority, retaining measured-stall/wakeup triggers and existing turn
  ceiling/pause/request checks. Non-weekly owner-requested `project_turns` rank as
  conversation; weekly synthesis ranks as supervisor. Project owner keys do not
  change with that priority class.
- Preserve each role's current scan order: experiments use Autopilot member
  priority then creation/ID; research uses updated time/ID; supervisor uses
  creation/ID. Across roles sharing a priority level, tie-break by creation time,
  source table, ID. Choose the highest-priority *eligible* proposal: one busy
  owner cannot hold up unrelated eligible work. No aging knobs or preemption.
- Typed owners are `('experiment', eid)` for stages/discussion/title/monitor and
  `('project', pid)` for research/supervision. Both live claims and saved
  dispatching/running/uncertain/received rows exclude a duplicate owner. Same-
  project experiment and project-role overlap is allowed, subject to existing
  control/evidence-revision result checks. Keep per-experiment active SQL guards.
- Authority is separate from priority and available capacity. Existing maintenance,
  pause, request, publication, control-revision, title/model/effort and turn-budget
  checks remain; an idle slot never creates new scientific or monitor work.

## Capacity decision selected by Opus

A new turn needs BOTH a free live connection and at least one unit of configured
turn capacity. Turn capacity is:

`max_sessions - live turn claims - unclaimed dispatching/running/uncertain rows`

Rows come from all three action tables and are identified by `(source_table,id)`;
count each action once. Received results, pending never-sent actions and terminal
rows consume no turn capacity, although received results retain owner exclusion.
A stale in-memory task is not proof an external turn ended. Session shrink keeps
all logical identities; do not create extra servers or silently discard debt.

Readback/reconcile/interrupt RPCs require no turn capacity and must remain able to
settle debt. Move existing recovery paths out of role-busy gates. An unrelated
owner progresses whenever both capacity counts have room; debt is not an owner
lock or a reason to block deterministic work globally.

This is stricter for multi-session restart recovery than the old global guards.
The source does not prove remote turns stop when an App Server exits. Opus chose
this conservative rule, but explicitly requires the owner to accept it before
implementation. Relaxing to logical-owner-only uncertainty requires the owner to
define `codex.max_sessions` as connection count rather than possibly-live account
turn count. No test may settle that product/authorization choice by assumption.

## Admission and quota boundaries

1. All seven existing admission sites deterministically prepare and propose jobs,
   not choose agents or immediately create tasks. In particular `prepare_workspace`
   completes before final selection. A proposal retains the existing source ID,
   role, owner, current authority and dispatch coroutine; no new persistent queue.
2. Selection, current eligibility and claiming owner plus connection happen in one
   synchronous section with no await. Claim before `create_task`/external calls;
   preserve the durable source action and persist dispatch intent before begin.
   Unknown outcomes never go back into a launch queue as a replacement turn.
3. Use one central quota-policy function: `gate.decide_policy` with run reserves
   for Autopilot members/runs, otherwise `gate.decide`; weekly synthesis also
   requires its existing plenty threshold. A cached selection filter is not
   authorization. Denial there claims nothing and preserves the existing hold.
4. After `refresh_quota()`, make the binding quota decision immediately before
   dispatch intent/begin, preserving authority/control-revision checks after any
   session-setup await. This is the one binding admission for that turn; role
   copies may be removed only after the pool owns both selection and dispatch
   boundaries. Binding denial releases the never-sent claim and leaves the source
   pending, with unchanged hysteresis/reserve semantics.
5. Every callback/reply/interrupt/readback/close remains routed through a's owning
   process/reservation. Terminal source application stays transactional with its
   events/outbox/obligations. No new schema, slot column, reason/config vocabulary,
   API-billed fallback or permission inferred from priority is authorized.

## Opus-selected concern-based split

Each proposed subcard stays below 500 changed source/test lines and green. A role-
based half-migration is unsafe because legacy flags still protect the primary.

- **T1.6b-1 (~450 lines):** proposal/selector/claim across all seven sites. Admit
  after deterministic advancement. Keep global guards as eligibility rules and
  derive legacy flags from the registry. No mixed-role progress claim yet.
- **T1.6b-2 (~300 lines):** central quota function at both boundaries; remove role
  copies only once covered. Test hysteresis, reserves and denial/release behavior.
- **T1.6b-3 (~450 lines):** capacity/owner exclusions and guard removal together,
  with minimum real-engine mixed-role tests; full recovery matrix remains c.

Within b-3, first enable/test capacity and owner exclusion with old guards still
present. Then separate deterministic advancement from inference and remove only:

- supervisor suppression of all experiment rows and conversation/title/controller;
- research suppression of supervisor recovery;
- research-busy guards in agent_stage/title dispatch;
- title/monitor/conversation global unresolved-action blockers;
- research lane waits/yields and supervisor global lane/unresolved waits;
- lane-local task checks replaced by assigned-slot occupancy;
- the global single-session SQL form, replaced by debt accounting (retain the
  per-experiment form).

No guard removal lands separately from its replacement owner/capacity rule. No
priority change preempts an active turn. Existing source triggers and result
application remain authoritative; pool idle time never launches extra work.

## Verification

Use T1.2b fakes, distinct process-generation logs and temporary SQLite. Assert:

- Two slots overlap experiment/research, experiment/supervisor, discussion and
  unrelated experiment on distinct connections; one slot and same owner serialize.
- Within-role ordering, conversation-class owner project turns and monitor rank
  remain correct; a blocked owner does not stall another eligible proposal.
- Cached admission cannot defeat fresh binding denial or a changed control
  revision. Denied/expired quota launches nothing and frees never-sent claims;
  Autopilot reserves and weekly synthesis plenty thresholds remain enforced.
- Unknown outcomes count once, cannot launch twice and survive restart/shrink;
  reconciliation proceeds even when no turn capacity remains.
- Every removed guard has equivalent owner/slot/capacity coverage, healthy jobs
  create zero additional monitor turns, and a's callback/answer routing remains.

Inspect source/action/thread/turn IDs, actual overlap and external call counts,
not just queue state. Run targeted role/lane tests, Ruff and full Python suite
outside restrictive subprocess sandboxes. No live acceptance or activation.
