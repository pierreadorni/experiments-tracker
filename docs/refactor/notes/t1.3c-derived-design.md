# T1.3c derived holds: completion design

Focused read-only design review, 2026-11-15, requested by the coordinator against
`ba018d7`. This note changes no runtime or live state. It refines the C2 rollout;
implementation cards still need coordinator review and measured size checks.

## Decision

Complete C2 by making obligations the saved execution-decision authority as well
as the responsibility ledger. The already-approved experiment-lifetime backend
`reconcile` obligation is the source for an advancement/admission decision that
has no separate source row. Its mutable wait/detail may record that decision.
This is the narrow missing capability; it does not require a table, migration,
configuration, new reason, or mutable action identity. Retaining the experiment
column as operational authority, or merely renaming its setter, does not finish c.

Use two deliberately distinct read APIs:

- **Execution decision:** read the current advancement decision (or terminal /
  cancellation decision described below), plus exact source predicates. This
  preserves existing admission, repair, resume and conditional-clear policy.
- **Compatibility hold:** select the highest-priority open responsibility using
  the existing C2 ranking, then map that responsibility and its validated decision
  to an existing reason. This is the read-only `hold_reason` on experiment and
  projection views. Normal running work and unblocked advancement map to null.

An execution gate must never consume the display winner: an unrelated owner
question or running turn may outrank a repair/submission blocker. C2 intentionally
changes display priority; it must not change permission to advance, retry or
submit. Do not scan lower-priority rows just to recover the old displayed value.

## Saved representation and ownership

1. Keep the lifetime advancement row backend-owned, kind `reconcile`, targeting
   the experiment ID. It survives source handoffs. Its detail has a small,
   validated JSON decision record, separate from all ordinary source detail.
   The record names an **existing reason** or null, the relevant phase/evidence
   generation, and an optional exact source reference. Validate reason/source/wait
   combinations against a closed mapping; never display arbitrary detail text.
   Error text stays in `experiments.error`; it is not a reason or identity.
2. `wait` names the actual blocker: an exact saved action/obligation, job intent,
   request/review, transfer, publication, steering identity, or an allowlisted
   quota/admission/backend condition. Source-less process decisions are facts
   about advancing this saved experiment, not fake jobs or fake agent actions.
   Store a quota snapshot ID or other existing policy evidence where available;
   absence of such evidence is not admission permission.
3. Ordinary action/source episode detail retains its established immutable
   phase/evidence identity. The compatibility mapper can join an exact source
   obligation to the advancement decision that references it; it does not write
   the presentation reason into that source's detail. Unrelated obligations do
   not inherit the advancement reason. The advancement row itself maps its
   validated decision when it wins; its ordinary empty decision maps to null.
4. Use existing owner-answer/approval obligations for pending requests and
   reviews. Pure admission waits remain backend responsibility unless a concrete
   owner action is required. For an owner-only policy decision without a request
   row, use an experiment-scoped `reconcile` responsibility tied to the saved
   decision generation and a saved inbox ID. Create/reuse the existing appropriate
   notice kind in the same transaction. A pause flag alone must not be labelled
   owner responsibility without the C2-required notice. Capacity, temporary
   availability and automatic quota refresh are backend work, not owner questions.
   A derived policy obligation is not another decision authority: it references
   and must agree with the canonical advancement decision.
5. One transaction changes source fields, decision/wait, any derived policy
   responsibility, error/phase, event, revision and outbox. The caller owns it.
   Re-read cancellation/archive and generation guards inside that transaction.
   Unchanged decisions leave obligation timestamps and experiment revision alone.
   A clear removes the matching decision, not arbitrary concurrent obligations.
   Conditional clears compare the current decision and exact source/generation
   under the same transaction. Existing unconditional clears keep their existing
   scope; they do not satisfy unrelated source obligations.

Do not persist tick heartbeats or rewrite decision detail on every admission
check. A changed blocker is a real wait transition; repeated denial is a replay.
The existing in-memory exception backoff remains unchanged, including restart
behavior. Saving `executor_error` does not turn it into a terminal failure or a
new durable retry schedule.

## Terminal, cancellation and archive boundaries

Before closing advancement on complete/failed, save its final decision, then
satisfy/abandon it in that same transaction. Terminal projection and failed-repair
selection can read this latest closed lifetime record. Historical terminal rows
without such a record retain their saved compatibility reason. Never reopen a
terminal obligation merely to recover a presentation value. A genuine approved
failed-to-preparing retry opens the next lifetime episode with its proven saved
phase transition; it does not mutate the prior terminal decision.

Cancellation is terminal for experiment advancement but not necessarily for
external effects. Close advancement and open a distinct backend `reconcile`
responsibility sourced by the saved experiment cancellation transition. It owns
`cancellation_pending` / `cancellation_unconfirmed`, exact outstanding action/job
identities and current cancellation error. Update it while settling cancellation;
clear its decision and satisfy it only when the existing cancellation procedure
establishes completion. Include filesystem checks already owned by that
procedure; no claim that absence of a source row proves external absence.
Cancellation projection uses this record ahead of ordinary terminal presentation.
This also works for frozen legacy cancellation, which has no advancement row.

Active legacy archival remains a deterministic policy of saved contract, phase
and deletion state (`archive_active`); return `legacy_contract_archived` without
creating advancement. Preserve the archive notice and state-change event using
saved notice/event evidence for replay idempotency, rather than the old column as
a marker. Archived update guards use this saved policy predicate, not the
computed display value. Exact legacy settlement/publication can still have their
own obligations. Terminal historical readers and immutable old event payloads
remain readable.

## Coverage required before activation

| Existing family | Canonical source / decision |
|---|---|
| Request expiry, needs attention, plan review | Exact pending-request or review generation; use exact inbox identity when attention has no request. |
| Agent dispatch uncertainty, continuation, session/prerequisite/budget failure | Saved action + phase/evidence and exact thread/turn when known; advancement records the policy decision after a terminal action. |
| Agent/compute pause, busy, quota, project/workspace/runtime/cluster availability | Advancement admission decision; saved experiment flags/policy/snapshot evidence where present. Running/uncertain source obligations remain open. |
| Submission/accounting, scheduler admission, resource unavailability, transfers | Exact job intent/transfer when present; advancement admission decision before an intent exists. Never create an intent for a refused admission. |
| Blocked implementation/agent stage, compute/validation failure, automatic repair | Saved action/stage/job identity and advancement decision; final failure decision survives closing advancement. Preserve current retry and repair limits. |
| Steering restart, identity wait/change, cancellation uncertainty | Saved steering request plus exact action/job; separate cancellation responsibility after experiment cancellation. |
| Publication pending, continuation after report correction, completion | Exact report request/version and advancement/final decision; delivery remains its separate durable responsibility. |
| Executor exception | Advancement decision + existing error/notice, unchanged in-memory backoff. |
| Autopilot authority/budget/accounting holds | Existing stage/attempt hold or saved run/member/control revision, referenced by the experiment advancement decision. No changes to project/run/discussion hold authorities. |
| Archive | Saved archival predicate and existing notice/event; no active advancement. |

The implementation must enumerate concrete existing strings, including dynamic
Autopilot status strings and interrupt reasons, against the final source baseline.
The table above specifies ownership, not an excuse to accept an arbitrary reason
parameter. Unexpected legacy reason strings are retained only through historical
read fallback; they do not become writable new vocabulary.

## Reader and writer migration without split authority

A safe staged rollout can be implemented with no new runtime flag:

1. Land inert decision/map helpers and the full b-source integration. Introduce
   a transaction-local reader for the *operational* decision, initially backed by
   the existing column. Port all raw reason SQL/gates to it before activation.
   Repair/recovery SQL should select phase/deletion candidates, then apply the
   exact predicate under the lock/transaction. Preserve ordering and revalidate
   before mutation. Do not introduce a SQLite UDF, view, cache or parallel SQL
   implementation of the mapper.
2. Replace writers by bounded groups with a transaction-local decision-transition
   helper. During the intermediate rollout it can maintain the old column as
   temporary authority and the inert new record together. Unconverted writers
   still own the old column; therefore **no reader switches early**. This period
   is not completion of c. Existing raw clears must become guarded transitions,
   including job-output observation's reason allowlist.
3. Once every group is ported, finalize bounded backfill under executor ownership
   before scheduling: re-read each candidate in its write transaction, seed the
   latest decision from the still-authoritative saved reason and exact source
   facts, and use the same mapping helpers as writers. No external calls or
   historical inventory. Include repair-eligible failures and outstanding
   cancellation, not just nonterminal experiments. Backfill conflicts are
   diagnostic; never manufacture episodes to bypass terminal-history conflicts.
   Reconcile intermediate inert decision records against that final saved state.
4. In one activation commit switch the shared operational reader to the canonical
   decision, enable compatibility derivation in experiment/projection readers,
   and remove the temporary column assignment from the helper. Remove
   `hold_reason` from `Store.update`'s allowed writable fields. The physical column
   stays as historical storage but no execution path writes or trusts it. The
   normal setup/start sequence must finish the bounded preparation before any
   workflow tick; new experiment creation establishes advancement transactionally.
5. CLI list, Autopilot member listing, legacy guards and every raw SQL consumer
   use the same computed/decision APIs for their respective purposes. Make read
   functions accept an existing transaction connection, or hold the store lock
   for a consistent source/obligation snapshot; never nest `Store.tx()` (it uses
   plain `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`). Read-only projection never backfills.

If the obligations table is absent, historical read-only views return their
stored presentation unchanged. Read-only schema-24 intermediate databases with
no decision record also need an explicit historical fallback; writable execution
after preparation treats a missing active advancement as a liveness defect,
never a reason to trust a stale column or authorize work. Terminal old rows may
always use their preserved historical reason. No down-revision live-binary
rollback is implied: a binary that still writes the retired column is not a safe
writer after activation; use a compatible forward fix or an owner-authorized
stopped restore with exact external reconciliation.

## Acceptance details

- Inject rollback after source/decision writes and before event/outbox completion;
  no partial source, obligation, revision or notice survives. Replay preserves
  identities, ages and event counts. Include error-only changes and clear-only
  changes.
- Exercise a priority collision: an owner question outranks an existing backend
  blocker for display, while admission/repair still obeys the saved decision.
  Ordinary active work projects null where its selected responsibility has no
  applicable hold. Never choose a reason by error text or owner rank alone.
- Prove late callbacks cannot erase a new generation's hold, override cancelled
  phase, or unfreeze legacy execution. Preserve job-output clear allowlist;
  preserve uncertainty across running/uncertain/running and failed repair.
- Exercise failure -> retry -> failure lifetimes, complete with late discussion,
  cancelled with unknown dispatch/job identity, cancellation after restart, and
  archive notification replay. Closed decisions remain immutable.
- Backfill keyset batches can restart without advancing timestamps, inventing
  episodes or dispatching. Reads on old schemas are write-free. Poison the old
  experiment column after activation and prove execution/derived readers ignore
  it for prepared current lifetimes.
- Watchdog inspects every open obligation, including terminal experiments' actual
  unsettled effects. Advancement delegates to its exact dependency; unblocked
  backend progress needs concrete service evidence, not existence alone. Keep a
  process-local serviced-at record from actual handler returns if existing saved
  evidence is insufficient; restart gives one existing watchdog interval of
  grace. Do not refresh obligation ages on ticks or let arbitrary events count as
  service. Check saved timer identity/due, not an invented polling timer. Reuse
  deduplicated `engine_stuck` notices and the existing interval; never dispatch,
  repair, retry or assert external absence from watchdog code.

No owner decision is needed for this refinement of named C2 capabilities. If the
coordinator instead forbids a validated mutable advancement-decision payload,
complete c is impossible under the stated no-schema/no-new-source constraint:
several current decisions have no other durable source. The minimal necessary
allowance would be exactly that payload on the approved lifetime obligation,
not indefinite retention of operational `experiments.hold_reason` writes.

Coordinator acceptance: approved as the bounded completion plan, with no runtime
activation from this note. The mutable validated payload is limited to the
experiment-lifetime advancement decision; ordinary source identity/detail stays
immutable. Each implementation must measure its own source/test budget, preserve
all current exact-identity guards and cover its reason family before activation.
Inert c1a may proceed in parallel with b-source wiring; c2/c3 and activation wait
for their stated dependencies. No owner approval or live authorization is added.
